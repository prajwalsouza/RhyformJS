How can a bowl become a saddle?

Keep the same grid of points. Change just one sign in the rule that gives them height.

Read the story without animation

Begin with the parabola y = x² / 3. Move copies along z and lift each by z² / 3: together they describe the bowl y = (x² + z²) / 3.

Change the plus to minus. The x-direction still curves upwards, while the z-direction now curves downwards. The surface becomes y = (x² − z²) / 3.

At the center, nearby points can be above or below the surface height. This is a saddle point, rather than a minimum.
